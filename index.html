starGaming Uploader

A private, single-user tool that publishes short gaming clips to the starGaming YouTube channel.

What it does

starGaming Uploader is a command-line script run by the channel owner on their own computer. It takes a finished, captioned 9:16 clip and uploads it to the owner's own channel (starGaming) using the YouTube Data API, setting the title, description, tags, category and audience in one step. It then records the upload in the owner's local spreadsheet.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no public website or app. The tool signs in with the owner's own Google account through Google OAuth.

YouTube API Services used

  • videos.insert to upload the owner's own videos
  • channels.list and playlistItems.list (read-only) to confirm the owner's channel and recent uploads

The tool does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users or channels.
