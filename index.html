starGaming Uploader

A private, single-user tool that publishes videos to the starGaming YouTube channel using YouTube API Services.

What it does

starGaming Uploader is a command-line script run by the channel owner on their own computer. It takes a finished video (gaming clips such as GTA VI and other games, and the owner's own original videos, including AI-generated videos, which are declared as altered or synthetic content) and uploads it to the owner's own channel (starGaming) using the YouTube Data API, setting the title, description, tags, category and audience in one step. It then records the upload in the owner's local spreadsheet.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no public website or app. The tool signs in with the owner's own Google account through Google OAuth.

YouTube API Services used

  • videos.insert to upload the owner's own videos
  • channels.list and playlistItems.list (read-only) to confirm the owner's channel and recent uploads

The tool does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users or channels.

Monetisation

The tool is free, shows no ads and sells nothing. The starGaming channel it uploads to may earn money through the YouTube Partner Program.
